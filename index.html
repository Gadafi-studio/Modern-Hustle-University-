[ Forex & Crypto · Level 1 · Lesson 1 of 13 ]

The Language
of Price

Before you place a single trade, you need to understand what price actually is. Not the number on your screen — but the mechanism behind it. Most retail traders lose money because they never learned this.

What OHLC Means

Every candle on your chart tells a story. Open, High, Low, Close — these four data points reveal the battle between buyers and sellers in a given time period.

Open = Price when the candle started High = Highest price reached Low = Lowest price reached Close = Price when the candle ended Bullish candle: Close > Open (buyers won) Bearish candle: Close < Open (sellers won)

Bid, Ask, and Spread

You never buy or sell at one price. There are always two prices: the Bid (what buyers will pay) and the Ask (what sellers want). The difference is the spread — this is how your broker makes money.

Why Price Actually Moves

Price doesn't move because of indicators or patterns. Price moves because of orders. Specifically, because large institutional orders — banks, hedge funds, central banks — need to be filled. Understanding this is the foundation of everything in this campus.

✓
Price = the last agreed price between a buyer and a seller
✓
Candles show you the battle — who won each period
✓
Spread = broker's commission — always factored into your trade
✓
Liquidity = where orders are waiting to be filled
✓
Institutions move price — retail traders react to it
