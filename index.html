08 — FAQ

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Do I need experience to join? +
No. Every campus starts from zero. The Forex campus assumes you've never traded. The App Building campus assumes you've never coded. What you need is discipline and willingness to ship.
How does payment work? +
One subscription covers all 5 campuses. Pay via M-Pesa, Paystack, or card. Cancel anytime. No contracts. Founding members lock in a lower rate.
How much time per week? +
Minimum 5 hours. Optimal 10–15 hours. One lesson drops on Wednesday, peer review is Sunday. The rest is you shipping your project.
What if I don't see results? +
Complete 80% of lessons, submit every project, post 0 wins after 90 days — we'll refund you. Zero people have claimed it.
